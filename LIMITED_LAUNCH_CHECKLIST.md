# Limited Launch Checklist

## Purpose

Run a small, private post-launch test to confirm that AI Source Hub is clear, useful, and stable before a wider public launch.

This stage is not a public announcement.

## Target Group

- 5-10 selected friends and close contacts
- Prefer testers who can give honest feedback on business tools, Arabic/English usability, and mobile experience

## Products To Test

- Business Idea Validator
- Startup Risk Scanner

## Test Coverage

- English experience
- Arabic/RTL experience
- Desktop experience
- Mobile experience
- Product input flow
- Result usefulness
- Copy/download report actions where relevant

## Feedback Questions

1. Was the platform's purpose clear?
2. Was the product easy to use without assistance?
3. Was the result useful for making a decision?
4. Was anything confusing, broken, or visually incorrect?
5. Would you use or recommend the platform? Why?

## Feedback Rule

Record feedback first; do not immediately implement every suggestion.

Classify each item, look for repeated patterns, then decide what must change before a wider launch.

## Feedback Classification

- Critical bug: Blocks use, breaks a core flow, causes incorrect behavior, or damages trust.
- Important improvement: Makes the product clearer, more useful, or easier to complete.
- Optional opinion: Personal preference, nice-to-have idea, or low-impact suggestion.

## Completion Criteria

The platform is ready for wider launch when:

- At least 5 testers complete one product without assistance.
- Business Idea Validator and Startup Risk Scanner each receive feedback from at least 3 testers.
- Arabic/RTL and English are both tested.
- Desktop and mobile are both tested.
- No unresolved critical bugs remain.
- Important repeated feedback has been reviewed and either addressed or intentionally deferred.
- The platform purpose is clear to most testers.
- Most testers find at least one result useful for making a decision.
