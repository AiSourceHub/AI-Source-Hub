# AI Source Hub - Project Charter

## Mission

AI Source Hub creates practical AI products that solve real business problems.

The mission is not to create prompts. The mission is to create focused, usable products that help people get a business result quickly.

## Product Rules

1. Every product solves one problem only.
2. Every product must be usable within 2 minutes.
3. Do not overwhelm the user with questions.
4. Focus on results, not explanations.
5. If something can be simplified, simplify it.
6. Build products step by step.
7. Every feature must create measurable value.

## Product Standard

Each AI Source Hub product should be:

- Fast: the user gets value in minutes.
- Focused: one job, one outcome.
- Practical: output can be used immediately.
- Measurable: the user can see whether it helped.
- Simple: no unnecessary inputs, screens, or explanations.

## First Product

Business Idea Validator

Goal: help entrepreneurs evaluate a business idea within 10 minutes.
