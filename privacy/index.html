Privacy Policy

AI Source Hub v1.0 is designed as a static, client-side product experience.

Information We Process

AI Source Hub products process user inputs locally in the browser for deterministic assessments. The current static version does not require accounts, passwords, payment details, or confidential financial records.

Analytics

Analytics integration points are prepared but disabled until real tracking IDs and a final consent approach are configured.

Data Storage

The current product experience does not include a platform database or saved cloud reports. Browser features such as language preference may use local browser storage.

Third Parties

No external AI provider, payment processor, or authentication provider is connected in v1.0.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@aisourcehq.com.
