# Startup Risk Scanner

Startup Risk Scanner helps founders identify the biggest risks facing a startup and choose practical risk-reduction actions.

## Intended User

The product is for founders, early operators, and small teams who need a quick decision-support scan before investing more time or money.

## Input Structure

The assessment uses 12 focused inputs:

- Startup stage
- Problem clarity
- Target customer clarity
- Evidence of customer demand
- Market size and accessibility
- Competitive advantage
- Business model clarity
- Pricing and revenue assumptions
- Founder or team capability
- Available runway in months
- Execution complexity
- Legal, operational, or dependency risks

It does not request confidential financial details.

## Risk Dimensions

The scanner scores six dimensions:

- Market Risk
- Customer Demand Risk
- Business Model Risk
- Competitive Risk
- Execution Risk
- Financial Risk

Arabic labels are included in the product content.

## Scoring Direction

Scores range from 0 to 100.

Higher scores always mean higher risk.

- 0-24: Low Risk
- 25-49: Moderate Risk
- 50-74: High Risk
- 75-100: Critical Risk

## Dimension Weights

- Market Risk: 20%
- Customer Demand Risk: 20%
- Business Model Risk: 15%
- Competitive Risk: 15%
- Execution Risk: 15%
- Financial Risk: 15%

The weights total 100%.

## Recommendation Logic

Recommendations are generated from the three highest-risk dimensions. Each recommendation includes:

- Risk title
- Why it matters
- Recommended action
- First validation step
- Priority
- Expected outcome

Recommendations are deterministic and evidence-oriented.

## Report Structure

The report includes:

- Product name
- Assessment date
- Overall risk score
- Overall risk level
- Executive summary
- Risk dimension breakdown
- Top three priority risks
- Strongest area
- Immediate next action
- Recommended actions
- Validation plan
- Disclaimer

## Supported Languages

- English
- Arabic

Arabic uses RTL layout and Arabic result copy.

## Known Limitations

- The scanner uses deterministic rules, not live AI.
- It does not perform market research.
- It does not verify external facts.
- It does not replace financial, legal, investment, or professional advice.

## Disclaimer

This result is a decision-support assessment only. It is not financial, legal, investment, or professional advice and does not guarantee startup success.
